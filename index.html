A bridge between Cambridge and Europe

We use innovation, technology and inclusive sport to create opportunity, tackle discrimination and build stronger links between Cambridge, the UK and Europe.

As a community interest company, everything we earn and build stays tied to that mission. With the UK back in Erasmus+ from 2027, we help young people, youth workers and coaches in Cambridge take part in European projects, and we welcome European groups to our city.

What we do

Every project brings together at least two of our three pillars.

Innovation

Digital and AI skills for young people and youth workers, and new tools that make activities easier to join.

  • Digital skills workshops
  • Training for youth workers and coaches
  • Open toolkits we create with partners

Inclusive sport

Grassroots and adapted sport as a way into learning, health and belonging, for every ability and background.

  • Mixed-ability sport sessions
  • Exchanges for grassroots coaches
  • Outdoor and active learning abroad

Inclusion

Reaching young people with fewer opportunities, and tackling racism, antisemitism and all discrimination.

  • Youth exchanges open to all
  • Anti-discrimination workshops
  • Support so cost is never the barrier

Working with Erasmus+

Erasmus+ is the EU’s programme for education, training, youth and sport. The UK rejoins it from 2027, so Cambridge organisations can once again take part in European projects on equal terms.

We start as a partner in projects run by experienced European organisations, then lead our own.

More about Erasmus+ in the UK

  1. Youth exchanges

    Groups of young people from several countries meet for a week or two of shared activities, sport and learning.

  2. Training for youth workers and coaches

    Courses and job shadowing abroad, bringing new methods back to Cambridge.

  3. Partnership projects

    Joint projects with European partners that produce lasting results, such as toolkits for clubs.

  4. Hosting in Cambridge

    Welcoming European groups to Cambridge with local clubs and youth services.
