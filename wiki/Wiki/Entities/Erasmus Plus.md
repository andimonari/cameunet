# Erasmus Plus

Erasmus+ is the EU's programme for education, training, youth and sport. The UK rejoins it from 2027, and [[Cambridge Europe Network CIC]] built its plans around that date.

The site names four kinds of activity: youth exchanges, training for youth workers and coaches, partnership projects, and hosting European groups in Cambridge. The three projects in development (Play Fair Lab, Digital Coach, Bridge Games) target the 2027 call and depend on funding decisions. They are planned with [[Tredu Club]] and [[ACPELIA]].
