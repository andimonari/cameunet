# Cambridge Europe Network CIC

A community interest company in Cambridge, England, short name CamEUnet. It uses innovation, technology and inclusive sport to create opportunity, tackle discrimination and link Cambridge with the rest of Europe. Its work is organised around the [[Three Pillars]].

As a CIC it has an asset lock, so its assets stay tied to the mission. It plans to join [[Erasmus Plus]] projects as a partner first, working with [[Tredu Club]] and [[ACPELIA]], and later lead its own.

The public website is the [[Index Page]]. Tagline: "Where Everyone Moves Forward." Contact: hello@cameunet.uk (as of 2026-11).
