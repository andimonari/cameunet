# Hero Arch

A full-width inline SVG in the hero of the [[Index Page]]. Pairs of polygons form a faceted arch that shifts from blue through white and red to gold, the same palette as the [[C Mark Logo]]. The C mark sits at the left end (Cambridge) and a navy box with gold EU stars at the right end (Europe). Under it, a caption reads "Cambridge, England" and "Europe: Austria, Cyprus and growing".

The polygon coordinates and colours look generated, but no generator script is in the repo (as of 2026-11). The polygons fade in on load unless the visitor prefers reduced motion.
