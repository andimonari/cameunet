# Three Pillars

[[Cambridge Europe Network CIC]] groups its work into three pillars, and every project combines at least two of them.

- **Innovation** (blue): digital and AI skills for young people and youth workers, and open toolkits.
- **Inclusive sport** (gold): grassroots and mixed-ability sport as a route into learning, health and belonging.
- **Inclusion** (red): reaching young people with fewer opportunities, and tackling racism, antisemitism and other discrimination.

Each pillar has its own brand colour on the [[Index Page]], and the same colours run through the [[C Mark Logo]].
